Privacy Policy

Chrp · Last updated 24 August 2026

Chrp is an app for organising amateur and recreational sports teams — scheduling games, collecting availability, and keeping a roster together. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, and how to get rid of it.

What Chrp collects

  • Your email address — used to send your sign-in link. Chrp has no passwords; a link to your inbox is how you sign in.
  • Your display name and jersey number — shown to other members of teams you belong to.
  • Your availability — whether you are in, out, or maybe for each event, and any blackout dates you set.
  • Content you write — announcements, replies, event notes, and sub requests.
  • A push notification token — so the app can send you event reminders.
  • Dues records — if your team manager uses the dues feature, the amount owed and paid.
  • Your location — only if you switch on automatic check-in, and only as described below.

About location

Location is off unless you turn it on. If you enable automatic check-in, Chrp asks iOS to tell it when you arrive at the venue for an upcoming game. Your position is not recorded as a history, not stored on our servers, and not shared with anyone. Chrp only writes that you checked in — the same thing that happens when you tap the button yourself.

Why Chrp collects it

All of it exists to make the app work: to sign you in, to show your team who is playing, to notify you about games, and to let your manager organise a roster. Chrp does not sell your data, does not use it for advertising, does not build profiles, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Who else can see it

  • Other members of your teams can see your name, jersey number, availability, and anything you post. Your blackout dates are visible only to you and your team's managers. Your dues record is visible only to you and your team's managers.
  • Google Firebase hosts the database, authentication, and server functions. Data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure.
  • Expo delivers push notifications, which requires passing your notification token and the text of the notification through their service.
  • Google Places receives what you type when searching for a venue, so it can suggest matches.

Nobody else. There are no analytics or advertising services in the app.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account from inside the app at any time: Profile → Danger zone → Delete account.

That permanently removes your profile, your availability history, your blackout dates, your dues record, and your membership in every team. If you are the last person on a team, that team and its events are deleted with you. If you are the only manager of a team that still has other members, you will be asked to make someone else a manager first, so their team is not left stranded.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Content you posted to a team may remain visible to that team where it forms part of a shared record, such as an announcement thread.

How long data is kept

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. Delete the account and it is removed. Backups held by our infrastructure provider may persist for a short period afterwards before being overwritten.

Children

Chrp is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information. Where a team includes younger players, accounts are expected to be held and managed by a parent, guardian, or coach.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected or who can see it, the updated date above will change and the new version will be published here.
